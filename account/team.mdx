**Manage Team** shares one WhatsApp number with other people. Only the number owner can invite teammates. Standard includes 5 seats. Premium includes 15. Seats are the people invited to the number, not dashboard logins on other numbers.

## Manage your team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    In your GreenTick dashboard, go to **Settings → Team Management**. This shows all current agents and their roles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a new agent">
    Click **Invite Agent**. Enter their name and email address. They will receive an invite email to join your GreenTick workspace. Seats used are shown at the top of this page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign roles and permissions">
    Each agent gets a role: **Admin** (full access including settings) or **Agent** (inbox and contacts only). Admins can manage billing and team members; agents cannot.

    | Role | Inbox | Broadcasts | Settings | Billing |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Admin | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
    | Agent | Yes | Limited | No | No |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create teams (groups)">
    Click **\+ New Team** to group agents, for example "Sales", "Support", "Billing". Conversations can then be assigned to a team rather than an individual. Useful when agents are interchangeable within a department.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove or deactivate an agent">
    Click the three-dot menu next to an agent's name and select **Remove**. Their conversations are not deleted. They remain in the inbox and can be reassigned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A teammate only sees the numbers they were invited to. Revoking a membership removes their access to that number. It does not delete contacts, templates, or chat history.

<Warning>
  **Manage developer app** lets that person create and revoke API keys for the WhatsApp Business account. Grant it only to people who should hold those keys.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  **Use Automations to auto-assign**

  Pair team setup with an [Automation](/automation/automations) rule, e.g., any conversation starting with "billing" is auto-assigned to the Billing team. Saves manual triage time.
</Tip>
