The AI bot answers customers on the connected number from the knowledge you provide. It is included on Standard and Premium.

Open **AI Bot**.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the bot">
    Add a bot for the phone number and describe how it should answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add knowledge">
    Add the facts, policies, and links the bot is allowed to use. The bot answers from that knowledge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test">
    Send a test question in the dashboard before you enable the bot on live chats.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable">
    Turn the bot on for the number. You can also assign a single conversation to the AI bot from **Live Chat** or from an automation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An [automation](/automation/automations) can hand a chat to the bot, and a teammate can take the chat back from [Live Chat](/messaging/live-chat).

<Warning>
  Enable the bot only after a test reply looks right. Customers see those replies on WhatsApp.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Keep the knowledge current. Update it whenever your pricing, policies, or products change, or the bot will answer from old facts.
</Tip>

The full written walkthrough is on [greentick.ai](https://greentick.ai/set-up-a-whatsapp-ai-bot/).
