The AI bot answers customers on the connected number from the knowledge you provide. It is included on Standard and Premium.

Open **AI Bot**.

<Info>
  **How the AI Bot works**

  GreenTick's AI Bot uses your uploaded knowledge base (FAQs, product info, policies) to answer customer questions in natural language. When it cannot answer, it hands the conversation to a human agent.
</Info>

## Set up the AI Bot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to AI Bot">
    In your GreenTick dashboard, go to **Automation → AI Bot**. Click **Enable AI Bot** to activate it for your connected WhatsApp number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your knowledge base">
    Click **Add Knowledge**. You can upload FAQs as a text file or paste them directly. The bot uses this content to generate answers. The more detailed your FAQs, the better the responses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure bot personality">
    Set a bot name, greeting message, and tone (formal/friendly). The greeting message fires when a new conversation starts and the bot is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set escalation rules">
    Define when the bot should hand off to a human: **Keyword trigger** (customer types "agent", "human", "help"), **Low confidence** (bot cannot answer), or **After X turns**. Escalated chats appear in the [Team Inbox](/messaging/live-chat) with a bot handoff tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test before going live">
    Use the **Test Bot** preview inside the dashboard to simulate customer messages. Check that answers match expectations and escalation triggers correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate and monitor">
    Toggle AI Bot to **Live**. Monitor bot performance in **Analytics → Bot Reports**: answer rate, escalation rate, average resolution time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An [automation](/automation/automations) can hand a chat to the bot, and a teammate can take the chat back from [Live Chat](/messaging/live-chat).

<Warning>
  Enable the bot only after a test reply looks right. Customers see those replies on WhatsApp.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Keep your knowledge base current**

  Outdated FAQs lead to wrong answers. Update the knowledge base every time your pricing, policies, or products change.
</Warning>
