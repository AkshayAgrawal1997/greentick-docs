Catalogs let customers browse products in WhatsApp and place an order in the chat. [Connect the WhatsApp Business account](/connect-whatsapp) first, then set the catalog up on **Catalogs**. Catalogs are included on Standard and Premium.

<Info>
  **What is the WhatsApp Catalog?**

  A WhatsApp Catalog is a storefront linked to your business number. Customers can tap "View Catalog" in any chat to see your product images, prices, and descriptions, and send inquiries in one tap.
</Info>

## Set up your catalog

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your Meta Commerce Manager">
    Go to [business.facebook.com/commerce](https://business.facebook.com/commerce) and create a Commerce Manager account. This is where your product catalog lives on Meta's side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your catalog to your WABA">
    In Meta Business Manager, go to **Commerce → Settings** and link the catalog to your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA). This makes the catalog available on your GreenTick-connected number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add products in GreenTick">
    In your GreenTick dashboard, navigate to **Catalog**. Click **\+ Add Product**. Fill in: product name, price, description, and upload an image (minimum 500×500px, JPG or PNG).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and verify">
    Click **Publish Catalog**. Open your WhatsApp number in a customer's phone (or use a test device). The catalog icon appears next to the chat input field. Tap it to confirm products are visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share catalog items in broadcasts">
    In [Bulk Broadcast](/messaging/broadcasts) or individual chats, click the **Catalog** icon to attach a product card directly to your message. Customers can view the full product and send a quote request in one tap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use a catalog

- Confirm Meta has granted the WhatsApp Business account access to the commerce catalog you want to use.
- Open **Catalogs** and review products for the connected number.
- Send a product, a product list, or a carousel from [Live Chat](/messaging/live-chat) or from an [automation](/automation/automations) while the customer service window is open.
- New catalog orders appear in GreenTick. The Developer API event is `order.received`.

Product changes in GreenTick are sent to the catalog connected for that phone number. Orders stay on the account even if you later hide a product.

<Info>
  If products do not appear after you connect WhatsApp, finish catalog setup on **Catalogs**. Connecting the number does not attach a catalog by itself.
</Info>
