Catalogs let customers browse products in WhatsApp and place an order in the chat. Connect the WhatsApp Business account first, then set the catalog up on **Catalogs**. Catalogs are included on Standard and Premium.

## Use a catalog

- Confirm Meta has granted the WhatsApp Business account access to the commerce catalog you want to use.
- Open **Catalogs** and review products for the connected number.
- Send a product, a product list, or a carousel from **Live Chat** or from an automation while the customer service window is open.
- New catalog orders appear in GreenTick. The Developer API event is `order.received`.

Product changes in GreenTick are sent to the catalog connected for that phone number. Orders stay on the account even if you later hide a product.

<Info>
  If products do not appear after you connect WhatsApp, finish catalog setup on **Catalogs**. Connecting the number does not attach a catalog by itself.
</Info>
