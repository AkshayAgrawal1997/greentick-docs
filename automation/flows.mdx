A Flow is a form that opens inside WhatsApp. Customers can register, book, or answer questions without leaving the chat.

Open **Flows**. Standard includes 5 Flows. Premium includes unlimited Flows.

<Info>
  **What are WhatsApp Flows?**

  Flows are interactive, multi-screen experiences built natively inside WhatsApp. Customers tap through screens (forms, selection lists, confirmation pages) without leaving the chat. Common uses: lead capture, appointment booking, order intake, feedback surveys.
</Info>

## Create your first Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Flows in the sidebar">
    Click **Flows** (lightning bolt icon) in the left navigation. You'll see your existing flows and their published status.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Click "Create Flow" and name it'>
    Hit **\+ Create Flow**. Enter a name (e.g. `Lead Capture Form`) and choose a category (Lead Generation, Appointment Booking, Customer Support, Survey, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design your screens">
    Each Flow consists of one or more **screens**. Add components to each screen:

    - **Text input**: name, email, phone fields
    - **Dropdown / radio buttons**: single or multi-select options
    - **Date picker**: for bookings
    - **Image**: add a visual header
    - **Footer button**: "Next", "Submit", "Confirm"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Success screen">
    The last screen should confirm the action. Add a thank-you message and a completion message. This is what the customer sees after submitting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the Flow">
    Once all screens are complete, click **Publish**. Meta reviews the Flow (usually under 2 hours). Published flows get a Flow ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the Flow to a template button">
    Go to **[Templates](/messaging/templates) → Create Template**. Under Buttons, select **Flow** as the button type and paste your Flow ID. Submit the template for approval. When sent to a customer, tapping the button opens your Flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Viewing Flow responses**

  All completed Flow submissions appear in the contact's conversation history. You can also export responses as CSV from the Flows section.
</Tip>

A published Flow can also be sent from a [broadcast](/messaging/broadcasts), an [automation](/automation/automations), or [Live Chat](/messaging/live-chat) during an open customer service window.

Submissions are stored on the Flow. You can review them in the dashboard. Deprecating a Flow stops new sends. It does not delete submissions you already have.

<Note>
  Flows are a Standard and Premium feature. If the page asks you to upgrade, the current plan does not include them. See [Plans](/account/plans).
</Note>
