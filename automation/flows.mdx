A Flow is a form that opens inside WhatsApp. Customers can register, book, or answer questions without leaving the chat.

Open **Flows**. Standard includes 5 Flows. Premium includes unlimited Flows.

## Create and publish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the Flow">
    Add the screens and fields on **Flows**. A new Flow is a draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publish the Flow before you send it. Drafts cannot be sent to customers. Meta only allows a delete while the Flow is still a draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Send a published Flow from a template, a broadcast, an automation, or the chat during an open customer service window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Submissions are stored on the Flow. You can review them in the dashboard. Deprecating a Flow stops new sends. It does not delete submissions you already have.

<Note>
  Flows are a Standard and Premium feature. If the page asks you to upgrade, the current plan does not include them.
</Note>
