GreenTick sends and receives through the WhatsApp Business account you connect. Contacts, templates, broadcasts, live chat, and API keys all belong to that account.

<Info>
  Starting from scratch on Meta? Complete [Meta setup](/meta-setup/create-business-portfolio) first: business portfolio, business details, and verification.
</Info>

<Info>
  **Before you begin**

  You need: an active Facebook Business Manager account, a phone number not already registered on WhatsApp, and a verified business profile.
</Info>

## Step-by-step: Connect your number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your GreenTick dashboard">
    Go to [dashboard.greentick.ai](https://dashboard.greentick.ai/) and log in with your registered email. If you don't have an account, click **Sign Up** on the login page to create one first.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Open Settings and go to "WhatsApp Account"'>
    In the left sidebar, click **Settings** (gear icon at the bottom). Then select **WhatsApp Account** from the submenu.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Click "Connect Number" and authorize with Facebook'>
    A Facebook login popup will appear. Log in with the account that owns your Facebook Business Manager. Grant GreenTick the required permissions when prompted. This allows GreenTick to send messages via the WhatsApp Cloud API on your behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your Business Account and add your phone number">
    From the dropdown, select the correct Facebook Business Manager. Then enter your phone number in international format (e.g. `+91 98765 43210`). Choose whether to verify via **SMS** or **voice call**.

    <Warning>
      Use a number that is **not** currently active on WhatsApp (personal or business app). If it is, you must delete the existing account from the WhatsApp app before proceeding.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the OTP and verify">
    You'll receive a 6-digit OTP on your chosen number. Enter it in the verification screen. Meta typically approves the number instantly once the OTP is verified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your number is now live on GreenTick">
    After verification, you'll be redirected back to the dashboard. Your number will show a **green "Active" status** in WhatsApp Account settings. You can now create [templates](/messaging/templates), build [broadcasts](/messaging/broadcasts), and receive messages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Display name matters**

  The business display name you set during this step is what customers see in their WhatsApp chats. Choose your official brand name. Changing it later requires Meta re-approval.
</Tip>

Catalogs are not part of this step. After the account is connected, set them up on **Catalogs**.

## Reconnect a disconnected number

If a number you already used is disconnected, click **Connect WhatsApp Business** again and select the same WhatsApp Business account. GreenTick restores that existing account, including automations, contacts, and other saved data, instead of creating a new one.

## After it is connected

From **WhatsApp Connections** you can:

- Sync the account with Meta when business details or numbers change
- Reconnect a number that lost its Meta session
- See each phone number on the account

The phone number you select in the dashboard is the line used by **Contacts**, **Templates**, **Broadcasts**, **Live Chat**, and **Developer**.

<Warning>
  Disconnecting or removing the WhatsApp Business account stops sends from that number. If a plan is cancelled, GreenTick may hold the account for a limited number of days before deletion. Read the notice on the connection before you confirm.
</Warning>
