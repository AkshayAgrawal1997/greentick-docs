GreenTick sends and receives through the WhatsApp Business account you connect. Contacts, templates, broadcasts, live chat, and API keys all belong to that account.

## Connect a new account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open WhatsApp Connections">
    In the dashboard, open **WhatsApp Connections**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Embedded Signup">
    Click **Connect WhatsApp Business**. GreenTick opens Meta's Facebook login. Choose the WhatsApp Business account that owns the number you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the sync">
    GreenTick loads the account and phone numbers from Meta. The number you select during signup is linked first. Add more numbers from that same account on this page afterward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Catalogs are not part of this step. After the account is connected, set them up on **Catalogs**.

## Reconnect a disconnected number

If a number you already used is disconnected, click **Connect WhatsApp Business** again and select the same WhatsApp Business account. GreenTick restores that existing account, including automations, contacts, and other saved data, instead of creating a new one.

## After it is connected

From **WhatsApp Connections** you can:

- Sync the account with Meta when business details or numbers change
- Reconnect a number that lost its Meta session
- See each phone number on the account

The phone number you select in the dashboard is the line used by **Contacts**, **Templates**, **Broadcasts**, **Live Chat**, and **Developer**.

<Warning>
  Disconnecting or removing the WhatsApp Business account stops sends from that number. If a plan is cancelled, GreenTick may hold the account for a limited number of days before deletion. Read the notice on the connection before you confirm.
</Warning>
