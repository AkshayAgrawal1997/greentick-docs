Contacts are the people you message from the connected phone number. A contact is unique by phone number on that number's WhatsApp Business account.

Open **Contacts** in the dashboard.

## Import contacts via CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title='Go to Contacts and click "Import"'>
    In the left sidebar, click **Contacts**. Then click **Import Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the CSV template">
    Click **Download Sample CSV** to get the correct column format. Required columns: `name`, `phone` (in international format, e.g. `919876543210` without the + symbol).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your data and upload">
    Populate the CSV with your contacts. You can add custom fields (e.g. City, Product Purchased) in additional columns. These become variables in your [templates](/messaging/templates). Upload the file and map each column to a field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm the import">
    GreenTick will show a preview of how many contacts will be added, updated, or skipped (duplicates). Confirm to complete the import. Large lists may take a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Other contact actions

- **Add a contact manually:** Go to **Contacts → + Add Contact**. Enter name, phone, and any custom fields. Useful for individual VIP contacts or testing.
- **Create and manage segments:** Use **Segments** to filter contacts by tags, custom field values, or broadcast engagement (e.g. "Opened last campaign"). Segments update dynamically. Use them to target specific groups in [broadcasts](/messaging/broadcasts).
- **View contact conversation history:** Click any contact to see their full message history, tags, custom field values, and which broadcasts they received. From here you can also start a direct conversation.
- **Handle opt-outs:** When a contact replies with opt-out keywords (STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE), they are automatically blocked from future broadcasts. You can also manually block/unblock contacts from their profile.

<Tip>
  **Phone number format**

  Always include the country code without the + sign. For India: `919876543210`. Missing or incorrectly formatted numbers will be skipped during import.
</Tip>

## Use contacts

- [Broadcasts](/messaging/broadcasts) can select saved contacts, filter them, import a file, or add numbers for that send. Contacts who opted out of broadcasts are not included.
- [Live Chat](/messaging/live-chat) opens the thread for a contact when they message you, or when you send them a template.
- Labels and custom attributes segment the list. See [Labels and attributes](/contacts/labels-and-attributes).

## Phone numbers

Store numbers with the country code and no spaces or symbols if you are preparing a file, for example `919876543210`. GreenTick normalizes numbers to digits, including the country code, before it saves them.

<Info>
  The same contact rules apply to the API. `POST /v1/contacts` creates contacts, and a duplicate phone number returns a conflict instead of a second record.
</Info>
