## Dashboard sections at a glance

| Section | What it does | Where to find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**Broadcasts**](/messaging/broadcasts) | Send bulk WhatsApp messages to your contact list | Left sidebar → Broadcasts |
| [**Contacts**](/contacts/overview) | Import, view, segment, and manage all your contacts | Left sidebar → Contacts |
| [**Templates**](/messaging/templates) | Create and manage your Meta-approved message templates | Left sidebar → Templates |
| [**Live Chat**](/messaging/live-chat) | Shared team inbox for incoming and ongoing conversations | Left sidebar → Live Chat |
| [**Flows**](/automation/flows) | Build interactive WhatsApp Flows (forms, surveys, menus) | Left sidebar → Flows |
| [**Settings**](/account/team) | Manage your account, number, team, and billing | Bottom of left sidebar |

## Key areas explained

<Steps>
  <Step title="Top navigation bar">
    Shows your active WhatsApp number, notification bell (for system alerts), and your account avatar (top-right) where you can access billing, profile settings, and logout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Left sidebar">
    Your primary navigation. Each icon maps to a feature. The **Live Chat bubble** shows an unread message count so you never miss an incoming customer reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Home / Overview page">
    Displays quick stats: total contacts, broadcasts sent, messages delivered, and recent campaign performance at a glance. Use this as your daily check-in view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settings → WhatsApp Account">
    Where you manage your connected number, view your API tier, check WABA (WhatsApp Business Account) status, and access phone number quality rating. See [Connect WhatsApp](/connect-whatsapp).
  </Step>
</Steps>

  New to GreenTick? Follow the [Quickstart](/quickstart) first.
</Tip>
