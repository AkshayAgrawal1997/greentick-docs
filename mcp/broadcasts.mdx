A broadcast sends one approved template to many contacts. The assistant does not schedule it until you have seen the recipient count and asked to schedule.

## Order of calls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use an approved template">
    `create_broadcast` takes `templateName` and `language`. The template must already be `APPROVED`. The result is a `PENDING` draft. It does not send. Pass `contactPhones` (up to 1,000 numbers with country codes), `labels` (label ids or names from `list_labels`), or both. A label includes every contact with that label except contacts who opted out. When the template has `{{name}}` placeholders, pass `variables` with those names as keys. When the header is an image, video, or document, pass `headerMediaUrl`. Pass `phoneNumberRef` when the account has more than one number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    `preview_broadcast` counts recipients. The assistant shows you that count. `schedule_broadcast` stays blocked until you accept it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    `schedule_broadcast` takes `scheduledDateTime`. The time is at least 5 minutes from now and within 30 days. A past time, a time inside the next 5 minutes, or a time past 30 days is rejected. The assistant calls this only after you ask.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`list_broadcasts` and `get_broadcast` read drafts and scheduled sends. `list_broadcast_deliveries` returns one row per recipient. Filter with `status`, `page` (starting at 1), and `limit` up to 200.

`delete_broadcast` deletes a broadcast that is still `PENDING` or `QUEUED`, and a finished broadcast that never had recipients. A broadcast that has started, was sent, or is inside the last minute before it starts cannot be deleted.
