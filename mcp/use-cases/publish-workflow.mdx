An automation runs on every WhatsApp number on the account. Do not attach a phone number. The assistant saves a draft first. Publishing runs only after `validate_workflow` passes and you ask to publish. A draft cannot enroll contacts.

## Ask this

```text
Show me starter workflows. Copy the one closest to a reply when a customer messages us, name it "Welcome reply", and validate it. Do not publish until I say so.
```

After you review the draft:

```text
Publish the Welcome reply workflow.
```

## What the assistant calls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy a starter">
    `get_workflow_catalog` lists starter workflows. `create_workflow_from_template` copies one by `templateSlug` and `name`. The copy is a draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate">
    `validate_workflow` checks the steps and does not save them. The first step must be a trigger: `message_received`, `webhook`, or `campaign_enrolled`. Name, description, text, and caption must fit `get_limits`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    `publish_workflow` runs only after you ask. A validation error stays on that tool. The assistant does not enroll the workflow to get around it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`create_workflow` and `update_workflow` store a draft you describe yourself, when you are not copying a starter. Enrollment of a published workflow is [Enroll an automation](/mcp/use-cases/enroll-automation). The call order is on [Automations](/mcp/automations).
