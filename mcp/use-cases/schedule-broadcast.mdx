A broadcast sends one approved template to many contacts. The assistant does not schedule it until `preview_broadcast` has returned a recipient count and you have asked to schedule.

## Ask this

```text
Create a broadcast draft for the approved template festive_offer in en to +14155550123 and +14155550124. Preview how many people will receive it and stop there. I will tell you if I want it scheduled.
```

After you accept the count:

```text
Schedule that broadcast for Friday at 10:00 in the account timezone.
```

## What the assistant calls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    `create_broadcast` takes `templateName` and `language`. The template must already be `APPROVED`. Optional `contactPhones` is a list of up to 1,000 numbers with country codes. Pass `phoneNumberRef` when the account has more than one number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    `preview_broadcast` counts recipients. The assistant shows you that count. `schedule_broadcast` stays blocked until you accept it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    `schedule_broadcast` takes `scheduledDateTime`. The time is at least 5 minutes from now and within 30 days. The assistant calls this only after you ask.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`delete_broadcast` can delete a broadcast that is still `PENDING` or `QUEUED`. A broadcast that has started, finished, or is inside the last minute before it starts cannot be deleted.

Field details are on [Broadcasts](/mcp/broadcasts).
