`send_ad_conversions` reports a conversion for chats that started from a click-to-WhatsApp ad. Catalogs and click-to-WhatsApp ads are on Standard and Premium. The assistant sends the event only after you ask. It does not treat a chat as a purchase on its own.

## Ask this

```text
List click-to-WhatsApp ads on this number. For the conversation with +14155550123, send a QualifiedLead conversion. Do not send a Purchase, and do not message the customer.
```

For a purchase, name the amount:

```text
Send a Purchase conversion for that same conversation, currency INR, value 1499.
```

## What the assistant calls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the ad chats">
    `list_ads` lists click-to-WhatsApp ads that started chats on this phone number. `list_conversations` supplies the conversation ids. The assistant does not invent an id.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the event">
    `send_ad_conversions` takes `eventName` of `QualifiedLead` or `Purchase`, and `conversationIds` with 1 to 50 ids. `Purchase` may include `currency` and `value`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This call does not send a WhatsApp message. Product messages in those chats are on [Send a catalog product](/mcp/use-cases/send-catalog-product). The tools are on [Catalogs and ads](/mcp/catalogs).
