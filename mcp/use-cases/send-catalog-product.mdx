Catalog and product messages go out through Live Chat, and only inside the 24-hour customer service window. Catalogs are on Standard and Premium. The assistant sends them only after you ask.

## Ask this

```text
List products in my catalog. In the open chat with +14155550123, send the product whose retailer id is SKU-104. Add the body "This is the one you asked about." If the chat window is closed, stop.
```

## What the assistant calls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the product">
    `list_catalogs` and `list_catalog_products` identify the catalog and the `productRetailerId`. The assistant does not invent a product id.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send inside the window">
    `send_product_message` takes the conversation `id` and `productRetailerId`. Optional fields are `catalogId`, `body`, and `footer`. `send_catalog_message` sends the connected catalog instead of one product, and it needs `id` and `body`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`send_product_list` and `send_product_carousel` cover several products in one message. A product that is not in the catalog yet is [Add a catalog product](/mcp/use-cases/add-catalog-product). Field limits are on [Inbox](/mcp/inbox) and [Catalogs and ads](/mcp/catalogs).
