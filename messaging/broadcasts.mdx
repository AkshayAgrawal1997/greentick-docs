A broadcast sends one approved template to the contacts you select on the connected phone number.

Open **Broadcasts** in the dashboard.

<Info>
  **Prerequisites**

  You need at least one **approved template** and at least one **imported contact** before creating a broadcast.
</Info>

## Create and send a broadcast

<Steps>
  <Step title='Go to Broadcasts and click "New Broadcast"'>
    In the left sidebar, click **Broadcasts**. Then click **\+ New Broadcast** in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your broadcast">
    Enter an internal name (e.g. `Diwali Sale Oct 2025`). This is only visible to you. It's not sent to customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a template">
    Choose an **Approved** template from the dropdown. The message preview will appear on the right. If you don't have an approved template yet, create one in the [Templates](/messaging/templates) section first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in variables (if any)">
    If your template uses variables like `{{1}}`, map them to contact fields (e.g. Contact Name, Custom Field 1). Each contact will receive a personalized message with their data filled in automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your audience">
    Choose recipients from:

    - **All Contacts**: your entire list
    - **Segment**: filtered group (by tag, custom field, or previous campaign behavior)
    - **Upload List**: a fresh CSV for this broadcast
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule or send immediately">
    Click **Send Now** to dispatch immediately, or toggle **Schedule** to pick a date and time. Scheduled broadcasts appear in the Broadcasts list with a "Scheduled" status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor delivery reports">
    After sending, open the broadcast to view real-time stats: **Sent, Delivered, Read,** and **Failed** counts. Click any stat to see which contacts are in that state.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Avoid broadcast spam**

  Only message opted-in contacts. High block rates damage your phone number quality rating and can restrict your daily messaging limit. Aim for relevant, value-first content.
</Warning>

After the broadcast starts, the list shows recipients and delivery. Failed messages can be retried from the broadcast when your plan includes that feature.

## Drip campaigns

Standard and Premium include drip campaigns. A drip enrolls contacts into an [automation](/automation/automations) whose trigger is **Enroll contacts**. Publish that automation and turn it on, then choose it for the campaign. Each campaign can use a different automation.

<Info>
  Broadcast volume counts toward the monthly message allowance on your plan. See [Plans](/account/plans).
</Info>

<Warning>
  Only message contacts who opted in. High block rates lower your number's quality rating and can cut your daily messaging limit. If every message in a broadcast fails, check that Meta has a card on file: see [Add a payment method](/meta-setup/add-payment-method).
</Warning>
