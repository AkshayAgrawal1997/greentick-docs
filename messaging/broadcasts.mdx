A broadcast sends one approved template to the contacts you select on the connected phone number.

Open **Broadcasts** in the dashboard.

## Send a broadcast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an approved template">
    The template picker lists approved templates only. If the template is missing, check **Templates** and wait for Meta approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill variables">
    Provide a value for each named variable. You can map variables from contact fields and attributes so each recipient gets their own value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select recipients">
    Search saved contacts, filter by labels or attributes, import a file, or add phone numbers for this send. Contacts who opted out of broadcasts stay excluded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or schedule">
    Choose **Send now** or **Schedule for a specific time**. Drip campaigns use **Start now** instead of **Send now**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the broadcast starts, the list shows recipients and delivery. Failed messages can be retried from the broadcast when your plan includes that feature.

## Drip campaigns

Standard and Premium include drip campaigns. A drip enrolls contacts into an automation whose trigger is **Enroll contacts**. Publish that automation and turn it on, then choose it for the campaign. Each campaign can use a different automation.

<Info>
  Broadcast volume counts toward the monthly message allowance on your plan. See [Plans](/account/plans).
</Info>
