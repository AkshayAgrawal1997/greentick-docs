**Live Chat** is the inbox for the phone number you have selected. New customer messages appear as conversations. A teammate can reply in the thread while the WhatsApp customer service window is open.

## Navigating the Live Chat inbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Live Chat in the sidebar">
    Click the **Live Chat** icon (speech bubble) in the left sidebar. The inbox lists all active conversations, sorted by most recent activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conversation views">
    Switch between:

    - **All**: every conversation across all agents
    - **Mine**: only conversations assigned to you
    - **Unassigned**: conversations waiting for an agent
    - **Resolved**: closed conversations
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a conversation to an agent">
    Open any conversation. In the right panel, click the **Assign Agent** dropdown and select a team member. They will be notified. You can reassign at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply to a message">
    Type your reply in the message box at the bottom. You can send:

    - Free-form text (within the 24-hour customer service window)
    - Images, documents, audio files
    - [Template messages](/messaging/templates) (outside the 24-hour window)
    - Quick replies (predefined shortcuts from your saved responses)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Label and resolve conversations">
    Add **labels** (e.g. Sales, Support, Complaint) to organize conversations. Once a conversation is complete, click **Resolve** to move it to the closed state. Resolved conversations can be reopened if the customer replies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **The 24-hour rule**

  After a customer sends you a message, you have a 24-hour window to reply with any content (free-form). After that window closes, you can only send a pre-approved template message to re-initiate the conversation.
</Info>

<Tip>
  **Set up Quick Replies**

  Go to Settings → Quick Replies to save commonly used responses. In Live Chat, type `/` to trigger the quick reply selector and respond faster.
</Tip>

You can assign a conversation to a [teammate](/account/team) from the inbox or from an [automation](/automation/automations). Full-screen live chat is available from the same inbox when you need more room.

## What creates a conversation

- A customer messages your connected number
- You send them a template from a broadcast, the inbox, an automation, or the API
- A catalog order or a click-to-WhatsApp ad starts a chat on that number

<Note>
  Delivery and read states come from WhatsApp. If a message fails, the thread shows the failure. Retry is available on broadcasts when the plan includes retry failed messages.
</Note>
