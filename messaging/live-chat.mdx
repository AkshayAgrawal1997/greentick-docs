**Live Chat** is the inbox for the phone number you have selected. New customer messages appear as conversations. A teammate can reply in the thread while the WhatsApp customer service window is open.

## Reply to a customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Select the thread in **Live Chat**. The history shows messages sent from the dashboard, broadcasts, automations, and the API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a session message">
    Text, media, and other session replies are available for 24 hours after the customer's last message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a template when the window is closed">
    After 24 hours, WhatsApp requires an approved template to continue the conversation. Pick an approved template and fill its named variables.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can assign a conversation to a teammate from the inbox or from an automation. Full-screen live chat is available from the same inbox when you need more room.

## What creates a conversation

- A customer messages your connected number
- You send them a template from a broadcast, the inbox, an automation, or the API
- A catalog order or a click-to-WhatsApp ad starts a chat on that number

<Note>
  Delivery and read states come from WhatsApp. If a message fails, the thread shows the failure. Retry is available on broadcasts when the plan includes retry failed messages.
</Note>
