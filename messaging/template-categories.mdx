Meta requires every template to have a category. The category decides how Meta reviews the template and what each message costs. A wrong category is a common reason for rejection.

## The three categories

| Category | Use it for | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Marketing** | Promotions, offers, product launches, re-engagement | Sale announcements, coupon codes, new arrivals, win-back messages |
| **Utility** | Updates about something the customer already did | Order confirmations, shipping updates, payment receipts, appointment reminders |
| **Authentication** | One-time passwords and login verification only | OTP messages, 2FA codes, account verification |

## Pick the right category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Does the message promote anything?">
    If it includes an offer, a discount, a product pitch, or brand promotion, it is **Marketing**, even when it is worded as a reminder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is it about a customer action?">
    If the customer placed an order, made a payment, or booked an appointment, and you are confirming or updating that, it is **Utility**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is it only a code?">
    If the whole message exists to deliver a code or verify identity, it is **Authentication**. Nothing else belongs in this category.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not submit promotional content as Utility to get an easier review. Meta checks the wording and can reject the template or move it to Marketing.
</Warning>

## Cost

Meta charges a different rate for each category. Marketing messages cost more than Utility messages, so keep post-purchase updates as Utility when they contain no promotion. Broadcasts and template sends also count toward your GreenTick plan's monthly allowance. See [Plans](/account/plans).

To create and submit a template, see [Templates](/messaging/templates). The full written guide is on [greentick.ai](https://greentick.ai/blogs/whatsapp-message-template-categories/).
