## The three template categories

| Category | Use for | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Marketing** | Promotions, offers, product launches, re-engagement | Sale announcements, coupon codes, new arrivals, win-back campaigns |
| **Utility** | Transactional and post-purchase updates | Order confirmations, shipping updates, payment receipts, appointment reminders |
| **Authentication** | One-time passwords and login verification only | OTP messages, 2FA codes, account verification |

<Danger>
  **Common mistake: Marketing content in Utility**

  Never submit a promotional message as Utility to avoid stricter review. Meta's AI detects promotional language and will reject or reclassify the template, which can impact your phone number quality rating.
</Danger>

## How to pick the right category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Does the message promote something?">
    If it includes any offer, discount, product pitch, or brand promotion, it's **Marketing**, even if it's disguised as a reminder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is it triggered by a customer action?">
    If a customer placed an order, made a payment, or scheduled an appointment, and you're confirming or updating that, it's **Utility**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is it purely an OTP or verification code?">
    If the entire message exists to deliver a code or verify identity, it's **Authentication**. Nothing else belongs in this category.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Pricing note**

  Different categories have different conversation costs. Marketing conversations are typically priced higher than Utility. For cost optimization, correctly categorize all post-purchase flows as Utility.
</Tip>
