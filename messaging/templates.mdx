A template is a WhatsApp message Meta reviews before you can send it. Broadcasts, automations, and the API can only send templates that are approved.

Open **Templates** in the dashboard.

<Info>
  **What is a template?**

  A WhatsApp template is a pre-approved message format. All outbound messages to customers (unless they messaged you first within 24 hours) must use an approved template.
</Info>

## Create a new template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Templates in the sidebar">
    Click **Templates** in the left navigation. You'll see all your existing templates and their status (Approved, Pending, Rejected).
  </Step>
  <Step title='Click "Create Template"'>
    Hit the **\+ Create Template** button in the top right. The template builder will open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set template name, category, and language">
    Give your template a lowercase, underscore-separated name (e.g. `summer_sale_offer`). Choose the correct category: Marketing, Utility, or Authentication. Select your language. See [Template categories](/messaging/template-categories).

    <Warning>
      Template names cannot be changed after submission. Use descriptive names you can recognize later.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your template components">
    Templates have up to 4 parts:

    - **Header** (optional): text, image, video, or document
    - **Body** (required): your main message. Use `{{1}}`, `{{2}}` for personalization variables
    - **Footer** (optional): short tagline or disclaimer
    - **Buttons** (optional): Quick Reply (up to 3) or Call-to-Action (URL / phone)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and submit for approval">
    Review the live preview on the right side. Once satisfied, click **Submit for Review**. Meta reviews templates. Approval usually takes a few minutes to a few hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check approval status">
    Return to the Templates list. Status will update to **Approved** (green), **Pending** (yellow), or **Rejected** (red). Approved templates are immediately available for [broadcasts](/messaging/broadcasts).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Variable tip**

  Always provide sample values for your variables during submission (e.g. `{{1}}` = "Priya"). Meta uses these samples to review your template content. Missing samples are a common rejection reason.
</Tip>

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ✅ Approved | Ready to use in broadcasts | Use it in your next campaign |
| ⏳ Pending | Under Meta review | Wait, typically resolves in minutes |
| ❌ Rejected | Did not meet Meta policy | Edit and resubmit, or check rejection reason |
| ⛔ Disabled | High user block/report rate | Review content quality, contact support |

## What you can include

- Header text, or a header image, video, or document
- Body text with named variables
- Footer text on marketing and utility templates
- Buttons: quick reply, URL, phone number, or copy code

Header images can also be generated in GreenTick. The monthly allowance depends on your plan. Trial accounts get a small number of generations for the whole trial. A failed or blocked prompt does not use one.

<Warning>
  Editing an approved template submits a new version to Meta. Do not send a template from a broadcast or the API until its status is approved.
</Warning>

Once a template is approved, send it from [Broadcasts](/messaging/broadcasts) or [Live Chat](/messaging/live-chat). A published [Flow](/automation/flows) can be attached to a template too.
