<Warning>
  **Why broadcasts fail without this**

  Meta requires a payment method before it allows business-initiated template messages ([broadcasts](/messaging/broadcasts)). If your broadcasts keep failing, this is the most common cause.
</Warning>

## Step-by-step: Add a payment method in Meta

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your GreenTick dashboard and open your number">
    Go to [dashboard.greentick.ai](https://dashboard.greentick.ai/). Go to **WhatsApp Connections**, open your number, and click **Manage** to open Meta Business Suite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Payment Method">
    In Meta Business Suite, navigate to the billing section. Click **Add Payment Method**. Choose **Debit or Credit Card** and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your card details">
    Fill in: name on card, card number, expiry month and year, and CVV. Accept the terms and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify with your bank OTP">
    Enter the OTP sent by your bank to verify the card. Click **Verify**, then **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your billing business information">
    Fill in your billing details: country, currency, time zone, business name, address, PIN code, GST number, and business purpose. This information is required by Meta for tax and compliance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="To remove or switch a card">
    To remove a card, click the three dots next to it and select **Remove**. To switch your default payment method, click the three dots and select **Set as default**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
