Meta bills template messages to the WhatsApp Business account. Until the account has a payment method, business-initiated templates, including [broadcasts](/messaging/broadcasts), fail.

<Warning>
  If broadcasts keep failing, check this first. A missing payment method is the most common cause.
</Warning>

## Add a card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your number from GreenTick">
    In [dashboard.greentick.ai](https://dashboard.greentick.ai/), open **WhatsApp Connections**, open your number, and click **Manage** to go to Meta Business Suite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a payment method">
    In the billing section, click **Add Payment Method**. Choose **Debit or Credit Card** and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter card details">
    Fill in the name on the card, card number, expiry month and year, and CVV. Accept the terms and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify with your bank">
    Enter the one-time code from your bank, click **Verify**, then **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add billing information">
    Fill in country, currency, time zone, business name, address, postcode, tax ID (GST number in India), and business purpose. Meta needs these for tax and compliance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove or switch a card

Click the three dots next to a card. Choose **Remove** to delete it, or **Set as default** to make it the main payment method.

<Info>
  This card pays Meta's message charges. Your GreenTick plan is billed separately in the dashboard. See [Plans](/account/plans).
</Info>
