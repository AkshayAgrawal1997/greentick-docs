<Info>
  **Before you begin**

  You need a personal Facebook account belonging to the business owner. Log in with that account, not a team member or manager's account.
</Info>

## Step-by-step: Create your business portfolio

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in with the Facebook account of the business owner">
    Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/) and log in with the Facebook account of the actual business owner, not a team member's or agency account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Meta Business Suite">
    Once logged in, you'll land on the Meta Business Suite home screen. If prompted, accept the terms and continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and set up your business portfolio">
    Click **Create account** or **Get started**. Enter your business name, your name, and your business email address. Click **Submit** to create the portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your Facebook Page to the portfolio">
    You'll be prompted to add a Facebook Page. Select an existing page or create a new one for your business. This links your page to the business portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your Instagram profile (optional)">
    You can connect your Instagram business account to the same portfolio. Click **Add Instagram account**, enter your credentials, and confirm. You can skip this step and do it later from Settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>
