A Meta business portfolio is the first step toward the WhatsApp Business Platform. Your WhatsApp Business account, verification, and billing all sit inside it.

<Info>
  Log in with the personal Facebook account of the business owner, not a team member's or agency's account.
</Info>

## Create the portfolio

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in as the business owner">
    Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/) and log in with the business owner's Facebook account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Meta Business Suite">
    You land on the Meta Business Suite home screen. Accept the terms if Meta asks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the portfolio">
    Click **Create account** or **Get started**. Enter your business name, your name, and your business email address, then click **Submit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your Facebook Page">
    Select an existing Facebook Page or create one for the business. This links the page to the portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your Instagram account (optional)">
    Click **Add Instagram account**, sign in, and confirm. You can skip this and add it later from **Settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
