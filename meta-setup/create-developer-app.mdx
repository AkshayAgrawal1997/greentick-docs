## Step-by-step: Create your developer account and app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Meta for Developers and click Get Started">
    Visit [developers.facebook.com](https://developers.facebook.com/) and click **Get Started**. You'll be prompted to register your developer account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your account by SMS OTP">
    Enter your phone number and confirm the SMS OTP Meta sends you. Then confirm your contact email address to complete registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your role">
    Select your role on the registration form. If you're unsure, choose **Other**. This does not affect your access to WhatsApp API features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create App and give it a name">
    On the developer dashboard, click **Create App**. Give the app a name close to your registered business name, for example, your company name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select use case: Other, then app type: Business">
    Under **What do you want your app to do?**, select **Other**. On the next screen, choose **Business** as the app type and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the app to your business portfolio and create it">
    In the **Business portfolio** dropdown, select the business portfolio you created earlier. Click **Create app**. Your app is now live and linked to your business.
  </Step>
</Steps>
