A Meta developer account and a Business app link your portfolio to the WhatsApp Business Platform. No coding is needed.

<Info>
  [Create the business portfolio](/meta-setup/create-business-portfolio) and [fill in business details](/meta-setup/fill-business-details) first.
</Info>

## Create the account and app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Meta for Developers">
    Go to [developers.facebook.com](https://developers.facebook.com/) and click **Get Started** to register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify by SMS">
    Enter your phone number and the SMS code Meta sends. Confirm your contact email to finish registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Pick your role on the form. If you are unsure, choose **Other**. The role does not limit WhatsApp features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the app">
    On the developer dashboard, click **Create App**. Use a name close to your registered business name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the use case and type">
    Under **What do you want your app to do?**, select **Other**. On the next screen, choose **Business** as the app type and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the portfolio">
    In **Business portfolio**, select the portfolio you created. Click **Create app**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
