Meta checks the details in your business portfolio during [business verification](/meta-setup/verify-your-business). Fill them in first.

<Warning>
  Your legal business name, address, and website must match your official documents exactly. Mismatched details are the most common reason verification is delayed or rejected.
</Warning>

## Add your business details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Meta Business Suite">
    Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/) and log in with the account that owns the business portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Business info">
    Click **Settings** in the left sidebar, then open **Business info**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the details">
    Click **Edit** and fill in every field: legal business name, country, street address, town or city, region, postcode, and business phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match your official documents">
    Type the legal name exactly as it appears on your registration or tax documents, such as a GST certificate or incorporation certificate. Small differences in spacing or punctuation can fail verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Meta uses these details during verification.
  </Step>
</Steps>
