## Step-by-step: Add your business details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Meta Business Suite">
    Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/) and log in with the Facebook account that owns your business portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Settings, then Business info">
    In the left sidebar, click **Settings**. Then open the **Business info** section from the settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit and fill in your business details">
    Click the **Edit** button. Fill in every field: legal name of business, country, street address, town or city, region, postcode, and business phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your legal business name exactly as on official documents">
    Type your legal business name exactly as it appears on your GST certificate, incorporation document, or other official records. Even small differences in spacing or punctuation can cause verification failures.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the details">
    Click **Save** after filling all fields. Your business info is now stored in the portfolio and will be used during Meta's verification review.
  </Step>
</Steps>
