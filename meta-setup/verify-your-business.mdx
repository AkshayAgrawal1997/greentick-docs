<Warning>
  **Before you begin**

  Your business details (legal name, address, website, phone) must already be saved in Meta Business Suite. Verification checks those details against public records. [Fill them in first](/meta-setup/fill-business-details).
</Warning>

## Step-by-step: Verify your Meta business

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Meta Business Suite and go to Business info">
    Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/), click **Settings** in the left sidebar, then open **Business info**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Business verification status and start verification">
    Scroll to the **Business verification** section. It will show _Unverified_. Click **Start verification** to begin the process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your business from Meta's matching records">
    Meta will search public business records and show matching entries. Select the record that matches your business. If no match appears, you can continue by uploading a document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your connection to the business">
    Meta will ask you to prove you represent the business. Choose a verification method: **email**, **phone call**, **SMS**, **WhatsApp message**, or **identity document**. The fastest method is usually email or phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the verification step">
    Follow the prompts for your chosen method. Enter the OTP or complete the identity check. Once confirmed, Meta will review your submission. This typically takes a few minutes to a few hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>
