Business verification confirms to Meta that your business is real. A verified business can move to higher WhatsApp messaging limits.

<Warning>
  Save your legal name, address, website, and phone in [Business info](/meta-setup/fill-business-details) before you start. Meta compares them with public records.
</Warning>

## Start verification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Business info">
    Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/), click **Settings**, then open **Business info**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start verification">
    In **Business verification**, the status shows **Unverified**. Click **Start verification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your business record">
    Meta lists matching public business records. Select yours. If none matches, continue by uploading a document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your connection to the business">
    Choose how to prove you represent the business: email, phone call, SMS, WhatsApp message, or an identity document. Email or phone is usually fastest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the check">
    Enter the code or finish the identity check. Meta reviews the submission, usually within a few minutes to a few hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>
