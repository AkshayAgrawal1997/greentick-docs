## Step-by-step: Set up your WhatsApp profile in Meta

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Meta Business Suite and go to Settings">
    Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/) and click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Accounts, then WhatsApp accounts">
    In Settings, click **Accounts**, then select **WhatsApp accounts**. Pick the account you want to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open WhatsApp Manager and select your phone number">
    Click **WhatsApp Manager**. From the list, select the phone number you want to update, then go to the **Profile** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your business logo as the profile picture">
    Click the profile picture area and upload your business logo. Use a square image for best results. Meta requires images to meet their quality guidelines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your display name and submit for Meta approval">
    Click **Edit** next to Display name. Enter your official brand name and click **Submit**. Meta typically reviews display name changes within a few hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set username and fill in business info">
    Add a username for your business (this appears in your WhatsApp URL). Then fill in: **category**, **description**, **address**, **email**, and **website**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Facebook Page and Instagram account, then Save">
    Scroll to the bottom and connect your Facebook Page and Instagram account to this WhatsApp number. Click **Save** to apply all changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
