Your WhatsApp profile is what customers see when you message them: display name, logo, and business details. You edit it in WhatsApp Manager after the number is [connected](/connect-whatsapp).

<Tip>
  A complete profile helps customers recognize you, which supports read rates and fewer blocks.
</Tip>

## Edit the profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/) and click **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open WhatsApp accounts">
    Click **Accounts**, then **WhatsApp accounts**, and pick the account to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the phone number">
    Click **WhatsApp Manager**, select the phone number, and open the **Profile** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    Click the profile picture and upload your business logo. A square image works best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the display name">
    Click **Edit** next to **Display name**, enter your official brand name, and click **Submit**. Meta reviews display name changes, usually within a few hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in business info">
    Add a username if it is offered for your account. Then fill in **category**, **description**, **address**, **email**, and **website**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link Facebook and Instagram, then save">
    Connect your Facebook Page and Instagram account to the number, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Choose the display name carefully. Every change goes back to Meta for review.
</Warning>
