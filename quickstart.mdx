This path takes you from a new account to a WhatsApp number you can message from.

## Prerequisites

- A Facebook account that can access the WhatsApp Business account you want to connect
- The business phone number you will use on WhatsApp
- A browser where you can complete Meta's Embedded Signup

## Create the account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Open [dashboard.greentick.ai](https://dashboard.greentick.ai) and create an account. You can also start from [greentick.ai](https://greentick.ai).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish onboarding">
    Enter the company details GreenTick asks for, including the business name, location, and how you plan to use WhatsApp. This creates your workspace before a number is connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect WhatsApp">
    Open **WhatsApp Connections** and click **Connect WhatsApp Business**. Log in with Facebook and choose the WhatsApp Business account. The number you select is linked first. You can add more numbers from that same account afterward.

    See [Connect WhatsApp](/connect-whatsapp) if Meta asks you to pick an existing account or a disconnected number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a contact">
    Open **Contacts** and add a customer, or use **Import** to upload a spreadsheet. Store the phone number with its country code, for example `919876543210`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or reply">
    If that contact has messaged you in the last 24 hours, open **Live Chat** and reply in the thread.

    To message someone who has not written recently, create a template, wait until Meta marks it **Approved**, then send it from **Broadcasts** or from the chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You are set up when **WhatsApp Connections** shows the business account and **Live Chat** can open a conversation for that number.
</Check>
